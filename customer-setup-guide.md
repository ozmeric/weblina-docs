# Getting set up with BuildFlow

*Weblina LLC · BuildFlow + Foreman AI. What you need, what it costs, and how we can host it for you.*

Your BuildFlow app runs on a few everyday services, and most of them are free. Your business information stays in **your own Google account**. You don't need to be technical: we do the setup with you on one short call.

## How the pieces fit together

```
Your app  ──►  Secure connector  ──►  Your Google account        +  Your Facebook Page
(website on       (passes requests,       (Sheet = your data, Gmail,     (and Instagram, where
```

The app and the connector need a place to live online. That's called "hosting", and the next section shows four ways to handle it, from fully hands-off to fully yours.

## Your setup call (about 20 minutes)

### Before the call

- Know which Google account the business uses, and have its password and your phone handy for the sign-in check.
- Make sure you can manage your Facebook Page, meaning you're listed as an admin.
- Have a credit card ready for Google Cloud billing (for the AI).

### On the call, you'll

- Sign in to Google and click **Allow** when the app asks for permission to use Gmail, Calendar, Sheets and Drive.
- Create the AI key and turn on billing, with us guiding each click.
- Add Weblina to your Facebook Page or Meta Business account.
- Choose a 4-digit PIN for opening the app.
- For options B or D only, create the account(s) and add us as a member.

### Your data, and if you leave

- Your leads, posts and project records live in a Google Sheet in your account. You can open or download it at any time.
- Emails are sent from your Gmail and events go on your calendar, so your history stays with you.
- If you stop working with us, we hand over the app files and help move the hosting to your own account.
